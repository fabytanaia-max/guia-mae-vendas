# Campaign URLs (Example)

Substitua `BASE_URL` pela URL publica final da landing.

## Base URLs
- Main offer: `BASE_URL/index.html`
- Low ticket: `BASE_URL/low-ticket.html`

## Meta Ads - Main Offer
- Variante A  
  `BASE_URL/index.html?utm_source=meta&utm_medium=paid_social&utm_campaign=ecom_pt_main&utm_content=video_before_after&utm_term=kit_12&price_variant=A`
- Variante B  
  `BASE_URL/index.html?utm_source=meta&utm_medium=paid_social&utm_campaign=ecom_pt_main&utm_content=video_before_after&utm_term=kit_12&price_variant=B`

## Meta Ads - Low Ticket
- Variante A  
  `BASE_URL/low-ticket.html?utm_source=meta&utm_medium=paid_social&utm_campaign=ecom_pt_low_ticket&utm_content=video_offer_direct&utm_term=mini_kit&price_variant=A`
- Variante B  
  `BASE_URL/low-ticket.html?utm_source=meta&utm_medium=paid_social&utm_campaign=ecom_pt_low_ticket&utm_content=video_offer_direct&utm_term=mini_kit&price_variant=B`
